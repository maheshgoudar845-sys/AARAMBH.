RETURNS POLICY

Every AARAMBH piece is checked before it ships. If a print issue still reaches you, report it within 3 days of delivery and we will look into it.

WHAT WE ACCEPT

  • Print peeling, cracking or flaking off the fabric
  • Print that is faded, patchy or visibly misaligned
  • Print that is missing, or clearly different from the product page

WHAT WE DON'T ACCEPT

  • Size or fit issues. Please check the size chart on the product page before ordering
  • Slight colour difference caused by your screen or lighting
  • Change of mind after delivery
  • Items that have been worn, washed or altered
  • Issues reported after 3 days of delivery

HOW TO REPORT AN ISSUE

  1. Check the delivery date. You have 3 days from the day your order arrives.
  2. Take clear photos of the print issue, plus a photo of the full product with the tag still attached.
  3. Fill in the request form below with your order details and upload at least 2 clear photos.
  4. We verify your photos before accepting the return, then contact you on WhatsApp or email with the next step.

QUICK ANSWERS

Can I return a product because the size doesn't fit?

No. Returns are only for print-related issues, so please use the size chart on the product page before you order.

Does this apply to Cash on Delivery orders too?

Yes. The same policy applies whether you paid online or chose Cash on Delivery.

What if I report after 3 days?

We can only consider reports made within 3 days of delivery, so please check your order as soon as it arrives.

What does delivery cost?

Delivery is ₹30 for Goa and Karnataka and ₹50 for the rest of India. Coupons are applied at checkout.
